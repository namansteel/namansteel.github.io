TMT full form: Thermo-Mechanically Treated

TMT stands for Thermo-Mechanically Treated. A red-hot steel bar coming out of the rolling mill is sprayed with water. The outside cools fast and becomes a hard, strong outer layer, while the core stays hot and cools slowly into a softer, ductile centre.

That combination is why TMT bars are used in RCC: the hard outer layer gives strength, and the soft core lets the bar bend without cracking. That matters during construction and in an earthquake. The ribs on the surface grip the concrete.

TMT grades: Fe 500, Fe 500D, Fe 550D

The number in the grade is the bar's minimum yield strength in N/mm². TMT bars in India are made to IS 1786.

GradeWhat it meansCommonly used for
Fe 500Min. yield strength 500 N/mm²Houses and general construction
Fe 500DSame strength, higher ductility ("D")Columns and beams; preferred by many engineers, and in earthquake-prone designs
Fe 550DHigher strength, high ductilityLarger and heavier structures

"D" grades have tighter limits on carbon, sulphur and phosphorus and must stretch more before breaking. Always use the grade written on your structural drawing.

Standard TMT bar length

The standard length of a TMT bar in India is 12 metres (about 40 feet). Bars are sold by weight, so the weight of one 12 m piece is useful to know (see the table below). At Naman Steel we can cut bars to the length you need.

TMT bar weight per piece and per metre

Weight per metre (kg) = d² ÷ 162, where d is the diameter in mm. For example, 12 mm: 12 × 12 ÷ 162 = 0.889 kg/m, so one 12 m bar weighs about 10.67 kg.

TMT bar HSN code and GST rate

TMT bars fall under HSN heading 7214 (bars and rods of iron or non-alloy steel, hot-rolled). Ribbed TMT bars are usually classified under 7214 20. GST on TMT bars is 18%.

The exact 8-digit HSN code can differ by product, so confirm it with your CA or check the code on your supplier's invoice. Every Naman Steel bill is a proper GST invoice (GSTIN 27AAHHM1061Q1ZT).

Best TMT bar for house construction

  • Follow your engineer's drawing for grade and sizes. For most houses this is Fe 500 or Fe 500D.
  • Buy BIS-certified bars only. TMT bars must carry the ISI mark under India's steel quality rules.
  • Choose a known brand that suits your budget. Major and local brands both make IS 1786 bars, and price differences come from brand, not standard.
  • Damp or coastal site? Some brands offer corrosion-resistant (CRS) TMT. Ask us.
  • Get a GST invoice and ask for the MTC (mill test certificate) for big orders.

How to check TMT quality and spot fake bars

  1. Look for markings on the barThe brand name, grade (e.g. Fe 500D) and ISI mark are rolled onto the bar at regular intervals.
  2. Verify the BIS licenceThe licence number (CM/L-…) can be checked in the official BIS Care app.
  3. Check the weightWeigh a full 12 m piece and compare it with the table above. Clearly underweight bars are a red flag. IS 1786 allows only small tolerances.
  4. Check the ribs and surfaceRibs should be uniform along the length. Avoid bars with heavy, flaky rust or cracks.
  5. Buy from a known dealer with a GST billUnusually cheap bars without a proper invoice are the most common source of fake TMT.
