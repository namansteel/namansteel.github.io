Price factors

What decides the TMT rate?

Two quotes for "12 mm TMT" can differ for good reasons. These are the main ones.

1. Brand

Major brands and local brands are priced differently, even when both meet IS 1786. Ask us for brand-wise rates.

2. Grade

Fe 500, Fe 500D and Fe 550D can be priced differently. Use the grade on your structural drawing.

3. Size (diameter)

Thinner bars such as 8 mm and 10 mm often cost a little more per kg than 12–16 mm bars.

4. Quantity

A few bars, a few tonnes or a full truckload: larger orders usually get a better rate.

5. Market movement

Rates follow the steel market, which moves with raw material costs and demand. That's why rates change daily.

6. GST, cutting & delivery

GST on TMT is 18%. Check whether a quote includes GST, and whether cutting, loading or delivery is extra.

How rates are quoted

Per kg, per quintal, per tonne

  • TMT is sold by weight, quoted per kg or per tonne.
  • 1 quintal = 100 kg and 1 tonne = 1,000 kg.
  • Quotes may be with or without 18% GST. Always ask.
  • To convert bars to kg, see the weight calculator. A 12 mm, 12 m bar weighs about 10.67 kg.
