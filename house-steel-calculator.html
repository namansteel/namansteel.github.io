What changes the quantity

Why the real figure can differ

  • Soil condition and type of foundation
  • Column spacing, beam spans and slab thickness
  • Number of floors planned now and later
  • Earthquake-zone design requirements
  • Extras like a water tank, staircase head room or balcony projections

Questions

House steel: common questions

How much steel is needed for a 1000 sq ft house?

As a rough guide, about 3.5 to 4.5 tonnes of TMT steel for 1,000 sq ft of total built-up area of an RCC-framed house. Your structural drawings give the exact figure.

How much steel per square foot?

A common rule of thumb for RCC houses is 3.5 to 4.5 kg of steel per sq ft of built-up area.

Which TMT grade should I use for my house?

Usually Fe 500 or Fe 500D, but always follow your engineer's drawing. See our TMT bar guide.
