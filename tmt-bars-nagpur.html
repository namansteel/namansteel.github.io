Which grade?

Fe 500, Fe 500D or Fe 550D

  • Fe 500: the most common grade for houses and general construction.
  • Fe 500D: "D" means higher ductility. The bar bends more before it breaks, which engineers prefer for columns and beams and in earthquake-prone designs.
  • Fe 550D: higher strength, used in larger and heavier structures.

Always follow your structural engineer's drawing for grade and size.
